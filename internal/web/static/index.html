Duplicates

Tracks that appear more than once. The best-quality copy is selected to keep. Clean up folds the extras into it in your library: its playlists and history switch over, play counts add up, and your cues move across, lined up to the new file. The extra files move to a _SuperSync Duplicates folder, so nothing is deleted, and it can all be undone.

Upgrades

Tracks where your only copy is below the quality bar, e.g. a SoundCloud rip. Buy them properly and rescan; they'll drop off this list.

SoundCloud login (optional)

Without a login, downloads are SoundCloud's stream (usually 128 kbps). With your login token, tracks whose artist turned on SoundCloud's download button come down as the artist's original upload instead, which is often a WAV.
  1. In your browser, log in at soundcloud.com.
  2. Open the developer tools (⌥⌘I on Mac, F12 on Windows) → Application (Chrome) or Storage (Firefox/Safari) → Cookies → https://soundcloud.com.
  3. Copy the value of oauth_token and paste it here. It's stored only on this computer.

Automatic sync

Keeps playlists added from SoundCloud or YouTube up to date while SuperSync is open: new tracks are downloaded and added to the same playlist.
SuperSync never quits rekordbox on its own. While rekordbox is open, changes wait until you close it, or until you click Restart rekordbox & apply.
